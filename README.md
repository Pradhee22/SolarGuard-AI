# 🌞 SolarGuard AI

## AI-Powered Solar Panel Monitoring and Fault Detection

SolarGuard AI is an AI-powered solar panel monitoring and inspection system designed to identify conditions that can affect solar panel performance.

The system combines electrical, environmental, and thermal monitoring with artificial intelligence to support smarter and faster solar panel maintenance.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Solar panels are continuously exposed to environmental conditions such as dust, leaves, bird droppings, and water droplets.

These conditions can reduce solar panel performance and energy generation. Manual inspection can also be time-consuming, especially for large solar installations.

SolarGuard AI aims to provide an intelligent monitoring and inspection solution that can help identify panel conditions and support timely maintenance.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

SolarGuard AI combines electrical and environmental monitoring with AI-based thermal image analysis.

The system monitors parameters such as:

- Voltage
- Current
- Power
- Solar irradiance
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Pressure

Thermal images of the solar panel can be processed using a CNN-based AI approach to classify panel conditions such as:

- Clean
- Dust accumulation
- Leaf fall
- Bird droppings
- Water droplets

The monitoring and AI results are displayed through a web dashboard to support maintenance decisions.

## 👥 Target Users

- Solar farm operators
- Solar panel maintenance teams
- Renewable energy companies
- Industries using solar power
- Solar panel owners

## 🤖 AI Approach

The thermal image captured from the solar panel is processed and analyzed using a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN).

The AI system classifies the panel condition and provides a classification result and confidence score for the dashboard.
